Presentation settings

The %% lines
%% play: Titlestarts a story
%% A -> B : tag | textmoves the dot, with a tag and a caption
%% A -> B, Csends two dots at once
%% skip A Bfades boxes this story doesn't use
%% wait 1500holds the picture for a second and a half

Each ## heading starts a slide; Present shows them one at a time, and the arrow keys walk each diagram's story before moving on. Pictures go in a box with img: "/icons/agent.svg". Tab indents; press Escape first to move on with Tab.
