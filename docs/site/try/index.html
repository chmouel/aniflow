Try it

Edit the Markdown and the preview redraws as you type. Nothing leaves your browser unless you copy a link.

The %% lines
%% play: Titlestarts a story
%% A -> B : tag | textmoves the dot, with a tag and a caption
%% A -> B, Csends two dots at once
%% skip A Bfades boxes this story doesn't use
%% wait 1500holds the picture for a second and a half

Pictures go in a box with img: "/icons/agent.svg". Tab indents; press Escape first to move on with Tab.
